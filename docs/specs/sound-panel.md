# Sound panel

Files: `sway/.config/sway/panels/sound.qml` (Quickshell), `sway/.config/sway/panels/sound/MediaCard.qml` (media card), `sway/.config/sway/panels/sound/sound.mjs` (lists, names, volume limit, media players and switching, track time), `sway/.config/sway/bin/media` (track in the bar, media keys), `sway/.config/sway/apps/waybar.jsonc` (`custom/media` module in the bar), `sway/.config/sway/panels/sound/toggle` (run by a click on the waybar volume icon and by the menu entry Setup > Sound).

## Purpose

The panel controls PipeWire directly: output and input volume, mute, the default devices and the volume of each app. A media card on top controls the player in use over MPRIS, and the bar shows what it plays. The detailed mixer, `wiremix`, is one click away for anything else.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The panel file parses, every type, property and import resolves against Qt and Quickshell, and Quickshell loads it in a headless Sway. Test: `tests/static.sh: qmllint`, `tests/static.sh: panels-load`
2. The Output and Input sections each show the default device with its name, a volume slider, the volume in percent and a live level bar. When a section has no device it says "No output device" or "No input device". Test: manual
3. Clicking a slider sets the volume, and the mouse wheel steps it by 5%. Volume stays between 0% and 100%. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/sound/sound.bats: "volume: stops at 0 and at 100%"`, manual
4. Clicking a section's icon toggles mute, and the icon changes to the muted one. Test: manual
5. When more than one output or input device exists, a list under the section picks the default, with a check mark on the current one. Devices are named by their PipeWire description, else nickname, else node name. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/sound/sound.bats: "names: description, then nickname, then node name; apps by application name"`, manual
6. The Apps section shows one volume row per app playing audio, named by the app, or "Nothing playing" when there is none. Recording streams, such as a call using the microphone, are not listed. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/sound/sound.bats: "lists: outputs, inputs, playing apps; recording streams and nodes without audio left out"`, manual
7. Up or Right raises the output volume by 5%, Down or Left lowers it by 5%, M toggles output mute, and Esc closes the panel. Test: manual
8. The media card on top shows the player that is playing, else one paused with a track, and is hidden when no player has a track. A browser player left stopped with no track is skipped. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/sound/sound.bats: "media card: the chosen player, else the playing one, else a paused one with a track; stale ones skipped"`
9. The card shows the player name, the cover (a music note when there is none), the title (the player name when the track has none), the artist, the album, and previous, play or pause, and next buttons, greyed out when the player does not allow them. Test: manual
10. When the player reports position and length, a progress bar with "position / length" follows the track every second while it plays, and a click on the bar seeks there when the player allows seeking. Times read m:ss, and h:mm:ss from one hour on. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/sound/sound.bats: "track time: m:ss, h:mm:ss from one hour on"`, manual
11. While a player has a track, the bar shows a play or pause icon with "Artist - Title" (70 characters at most, the whole text in the tooltip), grey when paused, and hides it otherwise. A click plays or pauses, a right click goes to the next track and a middle click to the previous one. Test: manual
12. When more than one player has a track, arrows beside the player name switch the card to the previous or next one, wrapping around. The pick also moves the bar and the media keys to that player, and lasts while it has a track, until another player starts playing. Every bar gets the pick at once, one per output on a machine with two monitors. The panel names the player as playerctl does, its D-Bus name without the MPRIS prefix, so the panel and the bar agree on the pick. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/sound/sound.bats: "media card arrows: next or previous player with a track, wrapping around"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/sound/sound.bats: "media card: the chosen player, else the playing one, else a paused one with a track; stale ones skipped"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/media.bats: "pick: that player wins while it has a track, a refresh is asked"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/media.bats: "status: another player starting to play ends the pick"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/media.bats: "pick: a picked player without a track is skipped"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/sound/sound.bats: "player instance: D-Bus name without the MPRIS prefix, as playerctl names it"`, manual
13. A click on the cover or the title brings the player's window to the front and closes the panel. Space plays or pauses the card's player. Test: manual
14. Without a pick, the bar and the media keys act on the first player that plays, else the first one paused with a track, the same order as the card. Pausing one player while another plays leaves the bar on the one that plays. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/media.bats: "status: the playing player wins over one paused after it, text and tooltip, Pango escaped"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/media.bats: "status: none playing = first paused with a track, stale player skipped; title alone without artist"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/media.bats: "toggle, next, previous: on the playing player"`
15. The bar text is cut at 70 characters with an ellipsis, and characters that Pango reads as markup (`&`, `<`, `>`) show as typed. With no player holding a track the module is hidden, also when the last player closes, and the keys fall back to playerctl's own pick. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/media.bats: "status: text cut at 70 characters, tooltip whole"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/media.bats: "status: no player with a track = empty text (module hidden)"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/media.bats: "toggle without a player holding a track: playerctl's own pick"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/media.bats: "status: the last player leaving (empty line) hides the module, no error"`
16. `media` with no argument, or `media pick` without a player name, prints the usage and exits 2. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/media.bats: "no argument: usage, exit 2"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/media.bats: "pick without a player: usage error, exit 2"`
