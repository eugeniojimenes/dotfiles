# Bluetooth panel

Files: `sway/.config/sway/panels/bluetooth.qml` (Quickshell), `sway/.config/sway/panels/bluetooth/flow.mjs` (click, pair and connect logic, plain JS so it runs under `qmltestrunner`), `sway/.config/sway/panels/bluetooth/toggle` (run by a click on the waybar Bluetooth icon and by the menu entry Setup > Bluetooth), the `bluetooth` module in `sway/.config/sway/apps/waybar.jsonc`.

## Purpose

The panel connects and disconnects paired Bluetooth devices, pairs new ones that need no code, and turns the adapter on and off. It reads BlueZ through `Quickshell.Bluetooth` and scans for nearby devices only while it is open. The panel registers no pairing agent, so a device that asks for a PIN or a passkey is paired through `bluetoothctl` behind the Advanced entry. Without an agent BlueZ only stores a pairing when `AlwaysPairable` is on, which `nixos/common.nix` sets.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The panel file parses, every type, property and import resolves against Qt and Quickshell, and Quickshell loads it in a headless Sway. Test: `tests/static.sh: qmllint`, `tests/static.sh: panels-load`
2. The waybar icon shows whether the adapter is off, on, or connected to a device. With a connected device that reports its battery, the icon also shows the battery percentage. Test: manual
3. The switch in the header turns the adapter on and off. With the adapter off the panel says "Bluetooth is off", with the adapter blocked by rfkill it says "Bluetooth is blocked", and without an adapter it says "No Bluetooth adapter". Test: manual
4. While the panel is open and the adapter is on, it scans for nearby devices and shows "Searching…". The scan stops when the panel closes. Test: manual
5. "My devices" lists the paired devices, connected ones first and then by name. A device that reports its battery shows the percentage. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/bluetooth/flow.bats: "lists: my devices connected first then by name, nearby by name and only with a name"`, manual
6. "Nearby" lists the unpaired devices that have a name, sorted by name. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/bluetooth/flow.bats: "lists: my devices connected first then by name, nearby by name and only with a name"`, manual
7. A click on a paired device that is not connected connects it, and the panel closes once the device is connected. A click on a connected device disconnects it and the panel stays open. A click on a device that is pairing, connecting or disconnecting does nothing. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/bluetooth/flow.bats: "paired device: panel closes once connected"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/bluetooth/flow.bats: "connected device: click disconnects, panel stays open"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/bluetooth/flow.bats: "busy device: click ignored"`, manual
8. A click on a nearby device pairs it, marks it as trusted and connects it. The pairing is stored (`Bonded: yes` in `bluetoothctl info`), so the device reconnects after a disconnect without pairing again. The panel stays open and shows the device under "My devices" once it is connected. Closing earlier would cancel the pairing, because BlueZ drops a pairing when the program that asked for it exits. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/bluetooth/flow.bats: "new device: link up mid-pairing never closes the panel, connect over D-Bus after Pair()"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/bluetooth/flow.bats: "new device: connected after pairing, panel stays open"`, manual
9. A failed pairing shows "Pairing failed. Put the device in pairing mode, or use Advanced (bluetoothctl) if it asks for a code". A failed connection shows "Connection failed". Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/bluetooth/flow.bats: "new device: pairing fails, message shown, nothing connected"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/bluetooth/flow.bats: "paired device: back to disconnected, Connection failed, panel stays open"`, manual
10. Hovering a paired device shows a forget button, and a click on it removes the pairing. Delete does the same for the selected paired device. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/bluetooth/flow.bats: "forget: pairing removed, flow dropped"`, manual
11. Up and Down move the selection, Enter acts on the selected device as a click would, and Esc closes the panel. Test: manual
12. The menu entry Setup > Bluetooth runs the panel's toggle script. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/menu/menu.bats: "entries: each runs its script or panel toggle"`
