# Menu

Files: `sway/.config/sway/panels/menu.qml` (the Quickshell panel), `sway/.config/sway/panels/menu/menu.mjs` (the tree, search and what each entry runs), `sway/.config/sway/panels/menu/toggle` (run by `Super + Space` in `sway/.config/sway/config.d/30-apps` and by a click on the waybar menu icon).

## Purpose

One menu holds the desktop's categories (Apps, Capture, Style, Setup, Toggle, System) and the entries at the top (Keybindings, Notifications, Clipboard history, Emoji), with every installed app under Apps. The whole tree is in memory, so typing searches every level below the one shown: an app or a setting is found from the top without opening its submenu, as in the Omarchy menu this one follows. It replaces a fuzzel menu where the entries jumped sideways while the app lines arrived and the search at the top did not reach the submenus. This spec covers the menu itself: navigation, search, the app list and how entries run. What each entry does is in the spec of its feature (bluetooth-panel, clipboard-history, hibernation, update and others).

## Acceptance criteria

1. The panel parses, every type, property and import resolves against Qt and Quickshell, and Quickshell loads it in a headless Sway. Test: `tests/static.sh: qmllint`, `tests/static.sh: panels-load`
2. The top level lists Apps first, then Keybindings, Notifications, Clipboard history, Emoji, Capture, Style, Setup, Toggle and System, and no app. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/menu/menu.bats: "root: the categories in order, Apps first, no app listed"`
3. Apps lists every installed application by name, sorted, with its icon from the icon theme, or a generic one without. An app is launched by its desktop id through `gtk-launch`, as one argument, so nothing from a `.desktop` file reaches a shell. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/menu/menu.bats: "apps: under Apps by name with their icon, launched by desktop id through gtk-launch, never a shell"`
4. Each entry runs its script or panel toggle. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/menu/menu.bats: "entries: each runs its script or panel toggle"`
5. Toggle › Power profile lists power-saver, balanced and performance with the current one checked, and a pick sets it and asks for the profile popup (`osd switch profile <name>`). Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/menu/menu.bats: "power profile: current one checked, a pick sets it and shows the popup"`
6. Style › Terminal opacity lists eight steps from 100% to 80% with the saved one checked. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/menu/menu.bats: "terminal opacity: eight steps, the saved one checked"`
7. Typing searches every entry below the menu shown, apps included, by label, and apps by their generic name too ("browser" finds Firefox). Every word typed must match, in any case. The menu's own entries come first, then a line, then the deeper ones with their path beside them ("Capture", "Style › Terminal opacity"). Within each group the better match comes first: the whole label, then the label's start, then a word's start, then anywhere. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/menu/menu.bats: "search: every level below, own entries first, deeper ones with their path, apps by name or generic name"`
8. Esc clears the search when there is one, otherwise goes up one level, and at the top closes the menu without running anything. Left or Backspace with an empty search also goes up one level, and Right opens the highlighted submenu. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/menu/menu.bats: "back: parent of a submenu, path of an entry"`, manual
9. Up and Down (and Tab, Shift + Tab) move the highlight, held at the first and last row; Page Up and Page Down move by a page. Enter runs the highlighted entry or opens its submenu, and the mouse highlights a row on hover and runs it on click. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/menu/menu.bats: "cursor: held at both ends, -1 without rows"`, manual
10. `Super + Space` and a click on the waybar menu icon open the menu in the middle of the focused output, and the same key or click closes it. The search field has the keyboard at once. Running an entry closes the menu. Test: manual
11. Every row keeps the same icon column, a glyph for the menu's entries and the icon image for an app, so no row moves sideways when the list fills or changes. The card keeps its size while typing. A submenu shows where it is ("Style › Terminal opacity  ← Esc") at the top right, and its entries have a `›` on the right. Test: manual

The window, the outside click and the workspace switch follow `docs/specs/panels.md`.
