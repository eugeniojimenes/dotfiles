# Notifications

Files: `sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications.qml` (Quickshell daemon: server, state, modes, IPC), `sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/Popups.qml` and `PopupCard.qml` (popups), `sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/Centre.qml` and `CentreEntry.qml` (notification centre), `sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/toggle` (opens the centre: bell click, `Super + Ctrl + ,`, menu), `sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/clear` (dismiss the popups or clear everything), `sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/notify.mjs` (rules), `sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/run` (started by `sway/.config/sway/config.d/90-autostart`), `sway/.config/sway/bin/dnd` (modes and the waybar bell), `sway/.config/sway/panels/common/PanelShell.qml` (the centre's window).

## Purpose

The daemon owns the freedesktop notification service in place of mako and draws the popups. It keeps each notification with its time and actions after the popup is gone, and the notification centre lists them, newest first, for the session. During a whole-screen share or a screen recording, popups are replaced by one card that only says how many notifications arrived and why they are hidden. Do not disturb hides everything, and battery warnings always get through.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The daemon file parses, every type, property and import resolves against Qt and Quickshell, and Quickshell loads it in a headless Sway. Test: `tests/static.sh: qmllint`, `tests/static.sh: panels-load`
2. A new notification shows its popup when no mode is on. During a whole-screen share or a recording it is counted in the hidden-notifications card instead, and with Do not disturb on nothing shows, even during a share. poweralertd's normal notifications (charge state changes) never show, and its critical ones (battery warnings) show through any mode. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/notify.bats: "delivery: share and recording hide behind the card, dnd silences, battery warnings pass any mode"`
3. The hidden-notifications card says "New notification" or "N new notifications", followed by "hidden while you share your screen" or, during a recording only, "hidden while you record the screen". It never shows an app name, title or text. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/notify.bats: "hidden card text: count, and the reason (share wins over recording)"`
4. A popup stays for the sender's timeout, 5 seconds when the sender leaves it to the server, and until clicked when the sender asks for no timeout or the notification is critical. The time counts from when the popup appeared: other popups arriving or leaving never restart it. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/notify.bats: "timeout: sender's value, 5 s by default, never for critical or 0"`, manual
5. Modes `dnd`, `screenshare` and `recording` are independent: turning one on or off never changes another, and unknown names are ignored. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/notify.bats: "modes: on, off and toggle per name, unknown names ignored"`
6. At most 50 notifications are kept after their popup is gone; beyond that the oldest are let go, never one still on screen. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/notify.bats: "history cap: oldest let go first, never one on screen"`
7. `dnd toggle`, `share-on`, `share-off`, `record-on` and `record-off` change the matching mode over Quickshell IPC and then send waybar signal 8. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/dnd.bats: "toggle, share-on, share-off, record-on, record-off: mode change over IPC, then waybar signal 8"`
8. `dnd status` prints the daemon's bell JSON as it comes, and empty text when the daemon is down or answers anything that is not JSON, such as `Target not found.` while it reloads. It never signals waybar: waybar runs it on that signal, so a signal from it would start a loop. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/dnd.bats: "status: the daemon's bell JSON, as it comes"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/dnd.bats: "status: daemon down gives empty text"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/dnd.bats: "status: daemon mid-reload answers 'Target not found.' with exit 0: empty text"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/dnd.bats: "status: never pokes waybar"`
9. The bell is crossed out while a mode is on, with the reason in its tooltip (recording, then screen share, then Do not disturb), and otherwise shows the number of notifications that arrived since the centre was last opened. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/notify.bats: "bar bell: mode wins (recording, share, dnd), else the unread count"`
10. poweralertd's charge state changes and transient notifications end with their popup and never reach the history; everything else is kept. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/notify.bats: "history: poweralertd state changes never kept, its warnings are"`
11. Each entry in the centre shows when it arrived: "now", minutes, hours, then the clock time from 12 hours on. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/notify.bats: "time in the centre: now, minutes, hours, then the clock"`
12. Popups sit in the top right corner of the focused output as a deck: the newest in front with the app name, summary, body (simple markup) and the app icon or image, older ones behind it with their top edges showing, up to three. A critical popup has a red border. With the pointer over the deck it fans out into a list, and no popup times out until the pointer leaves. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/notify.bats: "deck closed: front lowest, older edges a gap higher and smaller, up to three"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/notify.bats: "deck open: a list, newest on top, a gap between cards"`, manual
13. A left click runs the default action, or closes the popup when there is none; a right click dismisses the notification; other actions show as buttons that run them. Test: manual
14. If the daemon crashes, `run` starts it again within seconds, and nothing else answers for the notification service in the meantime (mako is not installed). Test: manual
15. During a whole-screen share, a new notification shows only the hidden-notifications card, which counts up with each one, closes on any click and after 5 seconds, and stays with a red border when one of them is critical. The notification itself is kept for the notification centre. Test: manual
16. A click on the bell, `Super + Ctrl + ,` or Notifications in the menu opens the centre on the focused output, and the same again closes it. Opening it clears the unread count and the popups on screen. Esc, a click outside or a workspace switch closes it. Test: manual
17. The centre lists the kept notifications, newest first, with icon, app, time, summary, body and action buttons, critical ones with a red border. A click on an entry runs its default action and closes the centre, an action button runs that action, the close mark dismisses the entry, and Clear dismisses all. With none, it says "No notifications". Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/notify.bats: "centre: kept ones only, newest first; meta line with or without app name"`, manual
18. The centre's Do not disturb chip turns the mode on and off, and while a share or recording mode is on a red line says so with a Show popups chip that turns that mode off and shows the list again. A middle click on the bell also toggles Do not disturb. The bell is grey when quiet, blue with new ones, yellow in Do not disturb and red while a share or recording hides popups. Test: manual
19. The modes on survive a restart of the daemon (saved in `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/sway-notification-modes`). Test: manual
20. The body keeps only bold, italic, underline, links and line breaks as markup. Any other tag, an image tag included, is escaped and shows as text, whatever spacing or nesting it uses, because Qt's styled text would fetch an image source over the network. A sender's text relayed by an app could otherwise make the machine contact any address, which leaks its IP and when the notification was seen. The app name, summary and action labels are drawn as plain text, so a tag in them shows as text too. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/notify.bats: "body: b, i, u, a and br stay markup, any other tag (img included, any spacing or nesting) shows as text"`, `tests/static.sh: plain-text`
21. `clear popups` (`Super + Ctrl + .`) asks the daemon to take the popups off the screen and keep them in the centre. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/clear.bats: "popups: dismissPopups over IPC, the centre keeps them"`
22. `clear all` (`Super + Ctrl + Shift + ,`) asks the daemon to dismiss every notification, as Clear in the centre does. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/clear.bats: "all: clearAll over IPC"`
23. With three popups on screen, `Super + Ctrl + .` removes them and the centre still lists them; `Super + Ctrl + Shift + ,` then empties the centre and the bell count. Test: manual
24. A new notification goes to the end of the history once per id: sent again with the same id, it moves there instead of showing twice. A sender replacing it in place (same id, new text, as chat apps do for a message count and poweralertd does for its battery warnings) counts as a new arrival too: it pops up again with the new text for its full time, and modes apply as to a new one. It counts as unread until the centre is opened. One carried over by a Quickshell reload stays in the history, never shows a popup again and is not unread. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/notify.bats: "arrival: end of the history once per id, unread after the centre opened, reload carry-overs silent and read"`, manual
25. During a share or a recording each new notification adds one to the hidden-notifications card, and a critical one keeps the card red. A transient notification or a poweralertd charge state change is let go at once when no popup shows it. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/notify.bats: "arrival: hidden ones counted, a critical one keeps the card red, ephemeral ones let go unless shown"`
26. `clear` with no argument prints the usage, exits 2 and sends nothing to the daemon. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/clear.bats: "no argument: usage, exit 2, nothing sent"`
27. While a share or recording mode is on, the centre hides its list, entries from before the share included, and shows only how many there are and why, such as "5 notifications hidden while you share your screen". Clear is hidden too. Opening the centre mid-share would otherwise put every entry on the shared screen. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/notify.bats: "centre hidden text: count and the reason, share wins over recording"`, manual
28. When the screenshare or recording mode turns on, the popups already on screen leave it and stay in the centre, so one that arrived just before a share or a recording never shows in it. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/notify.bats: "screen hidden: share and recording take popups off the screen, dnd alone does not"`, manual
29. A notification let go on arrival (a transient one, or a poweralertd charge change, that no popup shows) leaves the history at once and never counts as unread. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/notifications/notify.bats: "arrival: hidden ones counted, a critical one keeps the card red, ephemeral ones let go unless shown"`, manual
