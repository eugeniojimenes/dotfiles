# Screen share

Files: `sway/.config/sway/bin/share-picker` (what the portal runs: thumbnails, then the picker, then the pick back to the portal), `sway/.config/sway/panels/share.qml` and `sway/.config/sway/panels/share/share.mjs` (the picker, in Quickshell), `nixos/common.nix` (`xdg.portal.wlr.settings`: picker command, `exec_before` and `exec_after`; the portal service's `PATH`), `sway/.config/sway/bin/dnd` (`share-on` and `share-off`), `sway/.config/sway/bin/session-env` (sets `XDG_CURRENT_DESKTOP=sway`, so the wlr portal is the one asked).

## Purpose

When an app asks to share the screen (Google Meet, Slack, OBS), xdg-desktop-portal-wlr runs the picker, which shows monitors and windows as cards side by side, a window with a thumbnail of its content, with the look of the other panels. Sharing a whole monitor hides the notification popups until every share has ended, so a message never shows to the people watching. A shared window never shows popups, so it changes nothing.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The picker shows one card per monitor and window the portal offers, side by side: a monitor with a capture of what it shows now, its name and model, a window with a thumbnail of its own content, its title and its workspace ("workspace 5", or "scratchpad"). A window's thumbnail is taken with `grim -T`, so it shows the window even on another workspace or under another one; without it the card shows the app's icon. The captures are deleted once the picker closes. While a monitor is already shared or recorded, the picker takes no captures and every card shows the app's icon or a glyph, because the picker opens on the shared screen itself and would show other windows and the other monitor to whoever watches. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/share-picker.bats: "portal lines become cards: monitor name and model, window title and workspace, original line kept"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/share-picker.bats: "thumbnails: each window and each monitor as it is now, all removed after the pick"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/share-picker.bats: "monitor already shared or recorded: no captures, cards fall back to the app's icon"`
2. Picking a monitor turns on the screenshare mode, which hides notifications, then hands the portal its line back verbatim, so popups are hidden before the stream starts. It also remembers that a monitor was picked, in `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/sway-share-target`. Hiding at the pick covers a monitor share that starts while another share is live, when the portal skips `exec_before`. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/share-picker.bats: "monitor picked: line returned verbatim, pick remembered, notifications hidden before the portal gets it"`
3. Picking a window hands its line back, remembers that a window was picked and leaves the notification modes alone. A tab in the window title shows as a space on its card, and the line handed back keeps it. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/share-picker.bats: "window picked: line returned, pick remembered, notifications untouched"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/share-picker.bats: "window title with a tab: a space on the card, the tab kept in the line handed back"`
4. Closing the picker without a pick exits 1, and the portal cancels the share. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/share-picker.bats: "picker closed: exit 1, portal cancels the share"`
5. Once every share has ended, the portal runs `dnd share-off` and the popups come back. A Do not disturb set by hand stays on. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/dnd.bats: "toggle, share-on, share-off, record-on, record-off: mode change over IPC, then waybar signal 8"`, manual
6. In Google Meet, sharing the whole screen and then a single window opens the picker each time. Each window row shows its own thumbnail, also two windows side by side on one workspace; during the whole-screen share a `notify-send` shows no popup and the bell is crossed out, and after the share stops a new one shows. Test: manual
7. When a share starts and no other share is live, the portal runs `share-picker started` (its `exec_before`). It reads and forgets the last pick, and turns on the screenshare mode unless that pick was a window. With no pick on record it hides too. This covers Brave, whose "Choose what to share" dialog opens a preview share (the picker shows), ends it, and starts the real one without the picker, so `exec_after` runs between the two. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/share-picker.bats: "started: a monitor pick or none on record hides notifications, a window pick does not; each pick used once"`, manual
8. The cards sit in one row in the middle of the focused output, and the row scrolls when it is wider than the screen. Left and Right or the wheel move the selection, held at both ends, Enter or a click shares the selected card, and Esc or a click outside closes the picker without a pick. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/share/share.bats: "rows file: one card per row, the portal line keeps its own tabs, rows without a line dropped"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/panels/share/share.bats: "step: held at both ends, -1 without cards"`, manual
9. Brave asks for a window twice, once for its preview and once to share, and the portal can restore only a monitor pick. So a window pick is remembered, and the next ask within 60 seconds that still offers that window gets it back without the picker. The memory is used once: Brave's "Choose a different window" gets the same window on its first try and the picker on the next one. After 60 seconds, or when that window is gone, the picker opens. Test: `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/share-picker.bats: "window asked again within 60 s: the same window back without the picker, once"`, `tests/unit/sway/.config/sway/bin/share-picker.bats: "window asked again after 60 s, or no longer offered, or a monitor picked: the picker opens"`, manual
